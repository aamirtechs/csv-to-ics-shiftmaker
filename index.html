How it works

  • CSV and XLSX upload with column mapping for title, start, end, location and notes.
  • Timezone selection with ambiguous-date warnings and a preview before download.
  • Recurrence builder with count or until, validated against calendar rules.
  • Reminder offsets via VALARM and strict ICS escaping and line folding.
